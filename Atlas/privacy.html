Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 5, 2026

Atlas ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Atlas (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use Atlas, you may provide us with the following information:

  • User-Generated Topics and Subjects: Topic names and subject descriptions you create for learning
  • Quiz Responses: Your answers to quiz questions within lessons
  • Learning Preferences: Your chosen settings for depth (lesson count), teaching style, and interaction level
  • Reading Preferences: Font size, font family, text alignment, and reading interface settings
  • Goal Settings: Your daily lesson targets and learning goals
  • Achievement Progress: Your unlocked achievements and milestone tracking

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

Learning Progress Data:

  • Lessons completed and completion timestamps
  • Topics and subjects you've explored
  • Quiz scores and attempt history
  • Reading time and session duration
  • Streak data (consecutive days of learning)
  • Achievement unlocks and progress

Usage Analytics:

  • Feature usage patterns (which features you use most)
  • Navigation behavior within the App
  • Content generation requests
  • App crashes and error logs

Device Information:

  • iOS version
  • Device model and identifier
  • App version
  • Language and region settings
  • Time zone

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Apple App Store: Subscription status, purchase history, and transaction verification
  • Apple iCloud: If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 App Functionality

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized lesson content tailored to your preferences
  • Track your learning progress and maintain accurate streak counts
  • Deliver daily reminder notifications (if you enable them)
  • Sync your data across your Apple devices via iCloud (if enabled)
  • Unlock achievements and celebrate your milestones
  • Provide quiz functionality and score tracking
  • Remember your reading preferences and interface settings

2.2 AI Content Generation

Important Disclosure:

Atlas uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate personalized educational content. When you create a topic or request a lesson, the following information is sent to Anthropic's servers:

  • Your topic name (e.g., "Philosophy," "Quantum Physics")
  • Subject descriptions (if provided)
  • Your selected teaching style and interaction preferences
  • The lesson level you're requesting (Foundations, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, Mastery)

What This Means:

  • Your topic requests are processed by Anthropic according to their privacy policy
  • Anthropic may use this data to improve their AI models
  • We do not control how Anthropic processes this data

Learn More:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Anthropic Terms of Service: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/terms

You can minimize data sent to Anthropic by:

  • Using the on-device AI for topic validation (automatic for free users)
  • Choosing pre-generated topics instead of custom ones (when available)

2.3 Analytics and App Improvement

We use aggregated, anonymized usage data to:

  • Understand which features are most valuable to users
  • Identify popular subjects and topics to improve content recommendations
  • Optimize AI content generation prompts and quality
  • Fix bugs and improve app performance
  • Develop new features based on user behavior patterns

2.4 Advertising (Free Tier Only)

Google Mobile Ads Disclosure:

If you use the free tier of Atlas, we display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google collects and uses the following information for ad targeting and measurement:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS)
  • IP Address: Your approximate location
  • Ad Interactions: Which ads you view and click
  • Usage Patterns: App usage for contextual ad targeting

How to Opt Out:

  • Upgrade to Premium or Super Premium to remove all advertisements
  • Adjust your device's ad tracking settings in iOS Settings → Privacy → Advertising

Learn More:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We share your information with the following third parties:

3.1 Anthropic (Claude AI)

  • Purpose: AI-powered lesson content generation
  • Data Shared: Topic names, subject descriptions, teaching preferences, lesson level requests
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Control: You control what topics you create; avoid including sensitive information in topic names

3.2 Google LLC (Advertising)

  • Purpose: Ad serving and targeting for free tier users
  • Data Shared: Device identifiers (IDFA), IP address, ad interaction data, usage patterns
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Control: Upgrade to Premium/Super Premium to opt out entirely, or adjust iOS ad tracking settings

3.3 Apple Inc. (iCloud Sync)

  • Purpose: Cross-device data synchronization
  • Data Shared: All your Atlas data (lessons, progress, achievements, preferences)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Control: You control iCloud sync via iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud; disable to keep data local only

3.4 Apple Inc. (App Store Subscriptions)

  • Purpose: Subscription management and payment processing
  • Data Shared: Subscription status, purchase transactions, receipt validation
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Control: Manage subscriptions in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions

3.5 No Sale of Data

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data with the services listed above for the specific purposes described.

4. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Your data is retained as long as you have the App installed and your account is active
  • Learning Progress: Lesson completions, achievements, and streaks are stored indefinitely unless you delete your account
  • AI Generation Logs: Topic requests sent to Claude AI are logged for 30 days for quality improvement, then deleted
  • Subscription History: Stored for tax and legal compliance purposes for 7 years after subscription ends
  • Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all data is permanently removed within 30 days

5. Your Privacy Rights

5.1 General Rights

You have the right to:

  1. Access Your Data: View all your learning data in the App (Settings → Profile)
  2. Export Your Data: Export your progress, topics, and lessons (Settings → Export Data)
  3. Delete Your Data: Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Delete Account)
    • This action is permanent and cannot be undone
    • All lessons, progress, achievements, and preferences will be deleted
  4. Correct Your Data: Edit topics, subjects, and preferences directly in the App
  5. Opt Out of Ads: Upgrade to Premium or Super Premium to remove advertisements
  6. Disable iCloud Sync: Turn off iCloud sync in iOS Settings to keep data local only

5.2 California Residents (CCPA Rights)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  1. Right to Know: Request details about the personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  2. Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal exceptions)
  3. Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information, so no opt-out is necessary
  4. Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To Exercise Your Rights: Email us at atlas.learn.everything@gmail.com with the subject "CCPA Privacy Request"

5.3 European Residents (GDPR Rights)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  1. Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  2. Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data
  4. Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  5. Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  6. Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  7. Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  8. Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

Legal Basis for Processing:

  • Contract Performance: To provide App functionality and services
  • Consent: For optional features like notifications and iCloud sync
  • Legitimate Interests: For analytics and app improvement

To Exercise Your Rights: Email us at atlas.learn.everything@gmail.com with the subject "GDPR Privacy Request"

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

6.1 Encryption

  • Data in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Data at Rest:
    • Local data is protected by iOS device encryption and keychain
    • iCloud data is encrypted using Apple's iCloud encryption standards
    • We do not store credit card information (handled by Apple App Store)

6.2 Access Controls

  • No passwords or authentication tokens are stored in our systems
  • Subscription verification uses Apple's StoreKit secure validation
  • AI API keys are obfuscated and stored securely

6.3 Third-Party Security

  • Anthropic and Google maintain their own security practices for data you share with them
  • We regularly review third-party security certifications and compliance

6.4 Limitations

No system is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your device secure with passcode/biometric lock
  • Not sharing sensitive personal information in topic names or lesson content
  • Monitoring your device for unauthorized access
