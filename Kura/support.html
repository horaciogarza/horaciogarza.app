Thank you for using Kura. We are committed to providing you with the best experience. If you are experiencing technical issues, have questions, or wish to provide feedback, please use the resources below.

1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I get started with Kura?

Simply download the app from the App Store and follow the on-screen instructions to begin.

How do I delete my data?

Kura stores your data locally on your device. To clear this data, you can reset your data in the app settings or delete and reinstall the application.

Is Kura free to use?

Kura is free to download and use. Some features may require a subscription or in-app purchase.
