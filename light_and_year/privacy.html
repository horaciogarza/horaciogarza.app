Privacy Policy for Finger Pressure Last Updated: December 9, 2024 This Privacy Policy describes how Finger Pressure ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By downloading and using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. 1. Information We Collect 1.1 Information You Provide Game Center Profile: When you sign in with Game Center, we access your Game Center player ID and alias to enable leaderboards and achievements. This information is provided by Apple and does not include your real name unless you've configured Game Center to display it. Leaderboard Names: When you achieve a top score, you may enter a name to appear on the local leaderboard. This name is stored locally on your device only. 1.2 Automatically Collected Information Game Statistics: We collect and store the following gameplay data locally on your device: High scores Games played count Games won count Power-ups collected Survival times Play dates for consecutive day tracking Advertising Identifier (IDFA): With your permission (via App Tracking Transparency), we may collect your device's advertising identifier to show you personalized advertisements through Google AdMob. Device Information: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain device information, including: Device model and operating system version Device identifiers IP address General location information (country level) App usage data related to ad performance 1.3 Game Center Data When you use Game Center features, Apple collects and processes data according to Apple's Privacy Policy. This may include: Player ID Achievement progress Leaderboard scores Friends list (if you choose to connect with friends) 2. How We Use Your Information 2.1 To Provide Game Features Display local and Game Center leaderboards Track and display achievements Save your game progress and statistics Enable social sharing features 2.2 To Show Advertisements Display banner advertisements through Google AdMob Show personalized ads if you grant tracking permission Show contextual (non-personalized) ads if you decline tracking permission Measure ad performance and optimize ad delivery 2.3 To Improve the App Understand how the App is used Identify and fix bugs Improve gameplay and user experience Develop new features 3. Data Storage and Retention 3.1 Local Storage The following data is stored locally on your device: Leaderboard entries (names and scores) Game statistics User preferences Achievement progress This data remains on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data. 3.2 Game Center Storage Achievement and leaderboard data submitted to Game Center is stored by Apple according to their data retention policies. 3.3 Advertising Data Data collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's data retention policies. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services 4.1 Google AdMob We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may collect and use data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. This includes: Device identifiers IP address Usage data Advertising identifier (with your permission) For more information about how Google uses data, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites 4.2 Apple Game Center We integrate with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Apple processes this data according to their Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ 4.3 SKAdNetwork We use Apple's SKAdNetwork framework to measure ad campaign effectiveness while preserving user privacy. SKAdNetwork provides aggregated, anonymized data to advertisers without sharing individual user information. 4.4 No Other Third Parties We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties for their marketing purposes. 5. Your Privacy Choices 5.1 App Tracking Transparency When you first use the App, you'll be asked whether you want to allow tracking for personalized advertising. You can: Allow: Receive personalized ads based on your activity Ask App Not to Track: Receive contextual ads that are not personalized You can change this setting at any time: Open Settings on your device Scroll down and tap "Finger Pressure" Toggle "Allow Tracking" on or off 5.2 Game Center You can control your Game Center participation by: Signing out of Game Center in device Settings Managing your Game Center privacy settings in Settings > Game Center 5.3 Limited Ad Tracking Even if you allow tracking in our App, you can limit ad tracking device-wide: Open Settings Tap Privacy & Security Tap Tracking Toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" 6.
