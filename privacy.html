Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy & Cookie Policy explains how the website horaciogarza.app ("the Site," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when you visit it, and in particular how the Site uses cookies and Google AdSense to display advertising.

This policy covers the website only. Each iOS app published by HGarz Studios has its own separate privacy policy, linked from the app's section on the home page.

1. Information We Collect

The Site is a static portfolio website. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you choose to email us.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information (such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit) for security and operational purposes. In addition, our advertising partner (Google) collects information through cookies as described below.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide reporting and advertising information.

This Site uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Advertising cookies: Set by Google and its partners to serve and measure ads (see section 3).
  • Functional/analytics cookies: May be set by third-party services to understand how the Site is used and to keep it running reliably.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and you can set most browsers to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function as intended.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display ads on this Site. To do this, Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • These vendors may collect data such as your IP address, device and browser information, approximate (non-precise) location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to limit how often you see a given ad.

This data is collected and processed by Google as an independent data controller in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Your Advertising Choices

You can control the use of cookies for personalized advertising:

  • Opt out of personalized ads from Google in your Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising from many participating vendors at aboutads.info/choices (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU/EEA).
  • Manage cookies directly through your browser's privacy settings.

If you opt out of personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Consent for Users in the EEA, UK & Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we present a consent message (using a Google-certified Consent Management Platform) when you first visit the Site. Through it you can grant or refuse consent for the use of cookies and the processing of personal data for personalized advertising, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy and applicable law (GDPR/UK GDPR).

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your cookies and revisiting the Site, or through the consent controls where available.

6. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising shown is not directed to children.

We may update this Privacy & Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
