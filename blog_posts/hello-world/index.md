I have shipped a handful of iOS apps over the last couple of years — memory games,
a restaurant swiper, a collection tracker — and almost none of what I learned doing it
was written down anywhere. It lived in commit messages, in App Store rejection emails,
and in my own head, which is the least reliable of the three.

So: a blog.

## What goes here

Mostly the specific, annoying things:

- Swift and SwiftUI patterns that survived contact with a real app
- App Store Review, in detail — what got rejected, what fixed it
- The boring plumbing: CloudKit, StoreKit, Apple Health, background tasks
- Occasional detours into databases, because that is where I started

## What does not

Hot takes, framework wars, and posts that could have been a tweet.

If any of it is useful to you, that is a bonus. The first audience is me in six months,
trying to remember how I solved something.
