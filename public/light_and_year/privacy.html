Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 9, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Light and Year ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By downloading and using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Game Center Profile: When you sign in with Game Center, we access your Game Center player ID and alias to enable leaderboards and achievements. This information is provided by Apple and does not include your real name unless you've configured Game Center to display it.

Leaderboard Names: When you achieve a top score, you may enter a name to appear on the local leaderboard. This name is stored locally on your device only.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Game Statistics: We collect and store the following gameplay data locally on your device:

  • High scores
  • Games played count
  • Games won count
  • Power-ups collected
  • Survival times
  • Play dates for consecutive day tracking

Advertising Identifier (IDFA): With your permission (via App Tracking Transparency), we may collect your device's advertising identifier to show you personalized advertisements through Google AdMob.

Device Information: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain device information, including:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • General location information (country level)
  • App usage data related to ad performance

1.3 Game Center Data

When you use Game Center features, Apple collects and processes data according to Apple's Privacy Policy. This may include:

  • Player ID
  • Achievement progress
  • Leaderboard scores
  • Friends list (if you choose to connect with friends)

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To Provide Game Features

  • Display local and Game Center leaderboards
  • Track and display achievements
  • Save your game progress and statistics
  • Enable social sharing features

2.2 To Show Advertisements

  • Display banner advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Show personalized ads if you grant tracking permission
  • Show contextual (non-personalized) ads if you decline tracking permission
  • Measure ad performance and optimize ad delivery

2.3 To Improve the App

  • Understand how the App is used
  • Identify and fix bugs
  • Improve gameplay and user experience
  • Develop new features

3. Data Storage and Retention

3.1 Local Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Leaderboard entries (names and scores)
  • Game statistics
  • User preferences
  • Achievement progress

This data remains on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data.

3.2 Game Center Storage

Achievement and leaderboard data submitted to Game Center is stored by Apple according to their data retention policies.

3.3 Advertising Data

Data collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's data retention policies. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may collect and use data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. This includes:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Usage data
  • Advertising identifier (with your permission)

For more information about how Google uses data, visit: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4.2 Apple Game Center

We integrate with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Apple processes this data according to their Privacy Policy.

4.3 SKAdNetwork

We use Apple's SKAdNetwork framework to measure ad campaign effectiveness while preserving user privacy. SKAdNetwork provides aggregated, anonymized data to advertisers without sharing individual user information.

4.4 No Other Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Your Privacy Choices

5.1 App Tracking Transparency

When you first use the App, you'll be asked whether you want to allow tracking for personalized advertising. You can:

  • Allow: Receive personalized ads based on your activity
  • Ask App Not to Track: Receive contextual ads that are not personalized

You can change this setting at any time:

  • Open Settings on your device
  • Scroll down and tap "Light and Year"
  • Toggle "Allow Tracking" on or off

5.2 Game Center

You can control your Game Center participation by:

  • Signing out of Game Center in device Settings
  • Managing your Game Center privacy settings in Settings > Game Center

5.3 Limited Ad Tracking

Even if you allow tracking in our App, you can limit ad tracking device-wide:

  • Open Settings
  • Tap Privacy & Security
  • Tap Tracking
  • Toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete the information.

For users in the European Union, the minimum age is 16, or the applicable age in your country.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored in the App:

  • Game data is stored securely on your device
  • Network communications use encryption where available
  • We do not store personal information on our servers
  • Third-party services (AdMob, Game Center) use industry-standard security measures

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

The App may be used in countries around the world. When you use Game Center or view advertisements, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

9. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: You can request information about the data we collect
  • Right to Delete: You can request deletion of your data
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of the "sale" of personal information (though we do not sell data)

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

10. European Union Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: Request limited processing of your data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data
  • Object: Object to processing of your data

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: For showing personalized ads (you can withdraw consent at any time)
  • Legitimate Interests: For app functionality, analytics, and improvements
  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the game features you request

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify you through the App or other reasonable means if required by law
  • Obtain your consent if required by applicable law

Your continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

14. Data Deletion

To delete your data:

  • Local Game Data: Delete the App from your device. This removes all locally stored data including scores, statistics, and preferences.
  • Game Center Data: Manage through Game Center settings on your device or by contacting Apple Support.
  • Advertising Data: Managed by Google according to their retention policies. You can visit myaccount.google.com to manage Google data.

  • COPPA: This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
